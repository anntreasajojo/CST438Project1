# Project 01 Post Mortem - [07 / CST438 Project 1]

## Context
Our team wanted to build a nutrition tracking app that would let users make an account, log in, view their meals by different categories, add food, track nutrition information, be able to save their favorite foods and view charts of their daily and weekly meal logs. What we actually ended up shipping was a working prototype of this app with valid login processes and registration screens, a landing page, breakfast, lunch, and dinner screens, food categories, favorites functionality, a working database, the ability to add foods and nutrition tracking charts. Some of the features were not completed, such as the reminders feature and the snacks screen. However, the main functionality and UI of the app was completed.
